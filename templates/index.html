How FoodShare Works

A simple three-step journey from surplus food to meaningful redistribution.

🍲

1. Donate

Donors list safe surplus food with quantity, category, location and availability.

🤝

2. Match

Receivers discover suitable food using location, category, quantity and freshness.

💚

3. Redistribute

Requests are approved, pickups are scheduled and successful redistributions are tracked.
